2. The Pre-Shop Filter

Before buying anything, apply this non-negotiable checklist:

  • The Fit Test: Is it Slim or Tailored? (Reject all Regular, Relaxed, or Oversized)
  • The Shoulder Test: Do the shoulder seams sit perfectly at the edge of the shoulder?
  • The Palette Test: Does it belong to the Deep Autumn palette? (If unsure, skip)
  • Alteration Mindset: Buy for shoulders and length; the waist can be taken in later
  • Vertical Line: Does it create an unbroken vertical line? (Monochrome = elongating)

T-Shirts

  • Fit: Slim with structured fabric (avoid thin cotton)
  • Neckline: Crew neck or Polo. Shallow V acceptable
  • Sleeves: Mid-bicep to just above elbow
  • Length: 2–3 inches below belt line

❌ Avoid

  • Deep V or scoop necks
  • Logos and graphics
  • Oversized fits
  • Big checks, horizontal stripes
  • Light blue, bright white

7. Accessories & Grooming

⌚ Watches

  • Type: Analog only with clean dial
  • Size: 38mm to 40mm case diameter
  • Metal: Muted Gold or Brushed Warm Metal (Silver secondary)
  • Strap: Brown leather (daily), Black leather (formal)
  • Dial: Cream, Black, Navy

🧢 Hats

  • Styles: Flat cap, Structured baseball cap, Beanie
  • Colors: Brown, Olive, Charcoal, Navy
  • Avoid: Floppy shapes, bright/neon colors

✂️ Grooming

  • Hair: Styles with height on top (textured crop, quiff) to elongate. Avoid flat, slicked-back styles.
  • Facial Hair: Keep neat and structured (trimmed beard or clean-shaven). Avoid overgrown styles.
